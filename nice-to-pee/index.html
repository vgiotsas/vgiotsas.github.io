Nice to pee

Find a clean toilet nearby

Nice to pee is a free iOS and Android app that shows which nearby toilet is clean, safe and usable before you walk in. Every place gets an overall score and a separate score from women.

What you can do

  • See cafes, restaurants, bars, shops and public toilets on a map, coloured by score.
  • Filter for places that are free to use, open now, accessible, or have baby changing or a sanitary bin.
  • Rate a toilet on cleanliness, safety, privacy, supplies and waiting time, and add a short review or an entrance photo.
  • Add a place that is missing and save the ones you like.

You can browse without an account. You only sign in to rate, save, report or add a place.

Free, no ads

The app has no ads and does not sell your data. Read the privacy policy and the terms of use.
